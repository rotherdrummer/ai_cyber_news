HOW TO READ EACH ITEM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rating is machine-assigned triage against fixed published criteria, not a finding:
  - Red: Evidence of real-world AI-enabled malicious activity or AI agents acting beyond their
  sanction; a model rated at a critical cyber threshold; near-complete autonomous exploitation
  results; or an authoritative warning that the threat has materially changed.
  - Amber: A significant change that may matter soon: notable capability gains, new government
  guidance or warnings, changes to who can access cyber-capable models, or regulatory and
  institutional changes.
  - Green: Background: evaluation methods, general research, routine releases without cyber
  evidence, and commentary.
Source: 'Original source' means published by the organisation that did the work; 'Third-party
report' means reported by someone else and is weaker evidence. 'Source says' summarises what the
source reports.
