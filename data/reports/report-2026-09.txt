  (3) a UK or Five Eyes government warning or advisory on an AI-related threat that calls for
  action; (4) a frontier model rated at its developer's critical cyber level, or shown to find and
  exploit serious flaws in widely used software largely on its own; (5) a frontier AI agent taking
  unsanctioned actions on real systems outside its developer; (6) a major change to AI cyber
  regulation, law or official guidance that UK health and care must follow or is likely to follow,
  such as a UK bill or regulation passing a key stage, a UK regulator setting a new AI security
  requirement, new UK government AI security guidance, or EU AI Act security obligations taking
  effect.
  - Amber: A significant change that may matter soon: other real-world AI misuse and incidents,
  notable capability gains, new government guidance or warnings, changes to who can access
  cyber-capable models, or regulatory and institutional changes.
  - Green: Background: evaluation methods, general research, routine releases without cyber
  evidence, and commentary.
Source: 'Original source' means published by the organisation that did the work; 'Third-party
