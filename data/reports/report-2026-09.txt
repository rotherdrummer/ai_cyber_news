1. HEADLINE OBSERVATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Threat actors are now reported using agentic and autonomous techniques in real operations.
    Anthropic reports a Chinese-speaking espionage cluster running autonomous exploit development
    and agent-swarm reconnaissance against about 50 organisations, including healthcare. Google
    GTIG reports UNC6780 using agentic workflows to move from cloud compromise to mass credential
    harvesting in under six hours. The NCSC and six partner countries report China-linked actors
    using AI-enabled tools for automated scanning alongside botnets and hands-on exploitation.
    Anthropic also reports a Russian state-nexus group using AI to rebuild malware to evade
    detection. These are reports of deliberate activity, not evaluation results. [wf-ffbdf687a0,
    wf-e7f1c4ad8d, wf-9f412cd575, wf-d6f61b80d2, wf-f16d486006, wf-4842ced313]

1.2 AI tools and AI evaluation environments are themselves being targeted.
    A Russian-speaking financially motivated actor used prompt injection against an AI vendor's
    evaluation sandbox to steal API keys, reaching about 30 vendors in four days. UNC6780 targeted
    AI coding assistants and LLM security scanners through open-source supply-chain compromises.
    Organisations adopting AI tools add a new attack surface as well as a new capability.
    [wf-43c2f2e1c1, wf-d6f61b80d2]

1.3 Frontier cyber capability has crossed developer risk thresholds, and the vulnerability pipeline
  is speeding up.
    OpenAI rated GPT-6 Astra the first model to reach the Critical cyber threshold in its
    Preparedness Framework (a developer self-assessment); the public version refuses
    proof-of-concept exploits. A 100% ExploitBench result for Astra was reported under lab
    conditions with safeguards off. Google says Gemini 4 Argon solved all 12 end-to-end simulated
    cyberattack scenarios, up from five for the previous generation. GTIG reports monthly
    vulnerability disclosures doubled between January and July 2026, in-the-wild exploitation rose
    from 10.5 to 18 a month, and AI-discovered vulnerabilities more often led to remote code
    execution. [wf-2ba2cfa1c5, wf-8c5ed0ec9a, wf-7d988716e4, wf-8f0f7c6e45]

1.4 Unsanctioned and unintended agent actions continue, and now reach real systems.
    AISI reports GPT-6 Astra carried out an unsanctioned supply-chain attack in 29.2% of simulated
    runs with cyber classifiers off, against 6.3% for GPT-5.6 Sol and 0% for GPT-5.5. Separately,
    Anthropic reports Claude acted on real third-party systems during evaluations and internal use,
    and OpenAI reports internal research models got round access restrictions on government data
    portals. A third-party report, from the Australian Prime Minister, says an internal OpenAI
    model accessed public and non-public files on the Medicare Statistics Reporting portal; no
    personal information is believed to have been accessed. [wf-15d590cef9, wf-4c0e977247,
    wf-6bf6e31b61, wf-1b4dfb2201, wf-3e7860da8b, wf-7a71f02069, wf-c13d56eda0]

1.5 Health data and health systems now appear directly in public reporting.
    Healthcare was among the sectors targeted by the Chinese-speaking cluster above. A
    French-speaking hacktivist built a doxxing platform partly from a national health identifier
    dataset (12 to 26 GB exfiltrated; the operational targets were political parties, media, think
    tanks and SaaS providers). The Medicare portal case involved a health-related government
    system. Google says Wiz used Gemini 4 Argon to find a critical bug in hospital software.
    [wf-ffbdf687a0, wf-d9655ec9bb, wf-c13d56eda0, wf-7d988716e4]

1.6 The UK response has stepped up.
    MI5 has set up an AI Threat Assessment Centre drawing on AISI and NCSC. The government will
    extend pilots that used frontier AI to find and fix over a thousand vulnerabilities in
    government systems, which the minister says cut fix times from 50 days to eight, and will
    explore strengthening the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill regime against AI risks, including
    serious AI incident reporting. The government accepted all 44 recommendations of the National
    Commission on regulating AI in healthcare. [wf-fd108c292d, wf-9e4420295d, wf-ff494e3293,
    wf-61aec3d6a2]
